# Project Guidelines & Strict Rules

## Strict Design & Feature Prohibitions
1. **NO MARKETING FLUFF / NO VIP TAGS**:
   - NEVER add "VIP", "VIP Vault Access", "VIP Club", or similar tier tags to avatars, headers, or footers.
   - NEVER add newsletter capture strips, email/WhatsApp signup boxes, or marketing spam sections.
   - NEVER add "Marvz Coins", fake credit bars, or loyalty points systems unless explicitly demanded by the user.

2. **Core Store Focus**:
   - Keep designs clean, minimal, functional, and authentic to a high-end collectibles store.
   - Focus exclusively on: product catalog, search/filter, smooth cart drawer, and Lebanese delivery & BAU pickup logistics.
